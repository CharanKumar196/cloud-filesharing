**Last Updated:** June 30, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Cookies Policy ("Policy") explains how Cloud File Sharing System ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and application (the "Service").

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help us provide, protect, and improve our Service. By using our Service, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

## 2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small data files placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website. They contain information that is sent back to the server each time you visit.

### Types of Cookies by Duration
- **Session Cookies:** Deleted when you close your browser
- **Persistent Cookies:** Remain on your device until they expire or you delete them

### Types of Cookies by Purpose
- **Essential/Necessary Cookies:** Required for the Service to function
- **Preference/Functional Cookies:** Remember your settings and preferences
- **Analytics/Performance Cookies:** Help us understand how you use the Service
- **Marketing/Advertising Cookies:** Display relevant content and track campaign effectiveness

3. Manage cookies under "Cookies and Site Data"
